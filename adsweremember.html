    Activity 2 of 4 • The Anatomy

    The 4 Pillars of Visual Identity

    How did your brain instantly recognize those brands without reading full words? It comes down to four fundamental design pillars:

    1. Iconic Logo & Mark

    A simple, balanced mark scaling from a billboard to an app icon.

    2. Color Psychology

    Strategic colors evoking instant emotional associations before reading copy.

    3. Proprietary Shape

    Distinct packaging silhouettes like contour glass bottles and curved geometry.

    4. Sonic Audio Branding

    2-second audio earworms like the Netflix ta-dum or streaming chimes.
